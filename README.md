## The joke

> Dimas emptied 214 desks in four minutes flat — the best-run meeting of the quarter. There was no fire drill; he'd pulled the alarm himself to end the budget review.

## Why this joke

One named character — Dimas — instead of a collective "our office". The setup praises him with concrete specifics (214 desks, four minutes flat, best-run meeting of the quarter), and the final clause reverses it: there was no drill at all — he pulled the alarm himself to escape the budget review. The v1 entry ("The fire drill was our most productive meeting all quarter…") had the same scene but no single character and an ending that merely confirmed the premise.
